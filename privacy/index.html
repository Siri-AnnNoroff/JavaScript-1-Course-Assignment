1. Introduction

Rainy Days ("we," "our," "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use this site, how we use it, and the choices you have. This site is a student project created for educational purposes; no real payments or personal data are processed.

2. Information We Collect

When you browse our store, we may collect information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and shipping details entered during the simulated checkout process. We also store your shopping basket contents locally in your browser using localStorage, so your basket persists as you navigate between pages.

3. How We Use Your Information

Any information entered during checkout is used solely to simulate the order process and display an order confirmation. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties. Basket data stored in localStorage stays on your own device and is never transmitted to a server.

4. Cookies and Local Storage

This site does not use tracking cookies. It uses your browser's localStorage to remember the contents of your shopping basket between page visits. You can clear this at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

5. Third-Party Services

Product data displayed on this site is fetched from the Rainy Days API, provided for educational use. No personal data is sent to this API as part of browsing or basket functionality.

6. Your Rights

Since this is a simulated store, no real personal data is retained beyond your current browser session. You may clear your basket and any locally stored data at any time via your browser settings.
